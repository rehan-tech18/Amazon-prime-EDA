💡 Key Insights
Drama and Comedy are among the dominant genres.
Most titles have IMDb ratings concentrated around the 6–8 range.
Movies make up a larger portion of the content library.
Content production has increased significantly in recent years.
Production countries show differences in content volume and ratings.
